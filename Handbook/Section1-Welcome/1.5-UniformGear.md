## Class A Uniform

[Description of official Class A uniform]

- Scout pants or shorts
- Scout socks
- Scout belt and buckle
- Badges and insignia properly placed

**When to Wear:**
- Troop meetings
- Formal events and ceremonies
- Awards presentations

## Class B Uniform

[Description of Class B uniform - typically activity or troop t-shirt]

- Regular pants or shorts
- Casual but identifiable wear

**When to Wear:**
- Campouts and outdoor activities
- Service projects
- Informal meetings

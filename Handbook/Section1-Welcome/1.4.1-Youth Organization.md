# 1.4.1 Troop Organization - Youth Positions


## The Patrol Method

- Patrols typically have 6-8 scouts
- Patrol leader oversees patrol activities
- Patrols work together to accomplish various tasks. On Campouts, Patrols are a unit that must work together, eat together, and tent together. 

## Youth Leadership Positions

### Troop Level Positions
- **Senior Patrol Leader (SPL)** - Overall youth leader
- **Assistant Senior Patrol Leader (ASPL)** - Assists SPL
- **Scribe** - Records attendance and meeting minutes
- **Quartermaster** - Manages equipment and gear
- **Chaplain's Aide** - Helps Scout remain reverant, as they see fit
- **Troop Guide** - Helps younger scouts / patrols with Advancement and/or Skills development. 
- **Junior Assistant ScoutMaster** - A youth, typically of Eagle Rank, who assists the Scoutmaster.

### Patrol Level Positions
- **Patrol Leader (PL)** - Leads the patrol
- **Assistant Patrol Leader (APL)** - Assists the PL
- **Patrol Quartermaster** - Manages patrol gear


---

**Key Points:**
- Youth-led with adult support
- Leadership opportunities for all scouts
- How decisions are made at troop and patrol levels

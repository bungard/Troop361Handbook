## Who are we?
We can’t repeat it enough, Leadership-Duty-Knowledge; this is what Troop 361 is all about. Troop 361 was founded to promote these values and more by enabling boys to develop their character, their citizenship and their mental, moral, and physical fitness in a nurturing, spiritually fulfilling atmosphere.

Quality leadership is provided by well trained, motivated leaders who share a desire to keep scouting a vital part of the boy’s life.

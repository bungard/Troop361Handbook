# 1.2 Who are we?

## Scouting America

The mission of [Scouting America](https://www.scouting.org/) is to prepare young people to make ethical and moral choices over their lifetimes by instilling in them the values of the Scout Oath and Law.

Scout Law
A Scout is trustworthy, loyal, helpful, friendly, courteous, kind, obedient, cheerful, thrifty, brave, clean, and reverent.

Scout Oath
“On my honor I will do my best to do my duty to God and my country and to obey the Scout Law; to help other people at all times; to keep myself physically strong, mentally awake, and morally straight.”

## Great Trail Council

[Great Trail Council](https://www.gtcscouting.org/) is the local council that Troop361 operates within. "Council" helps facilitates numerous administrative and functional programming to assist the Troop. 

The council is subdivided into districts, our Troop is supported by [Canal District](https://www.gtcscouting.org/about/districts-of-the-great-trail-council/canal-district).

## Troop 361

The unit charter for Troop361 is granted by Great Trail Council to First Congregational Church of Tallmadge. As such, the "Church" provides support to the troop to serve out its mission. 
A Chartered Organization Representative is designated by the leadership of the church to be the liasion between the Troop Committee and the Church itself.

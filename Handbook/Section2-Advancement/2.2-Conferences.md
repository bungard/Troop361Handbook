# 2.2 The Scoutmaster Conference & Board of Review

## Scoutmaster Conference

### Purpose
The Scoutmaster Conference is a one-on-one discussion to review progress, celebrate achievements, and provide guidance for continued growth.

### What to Expect:
- Casual conversation with the Scoutmaster
- Review of rank requirements completed
- Discussion of leadership and personal growth
- Next steps and goals
- Typically 15-30 minutes

### How to Prepare:
- [ ] Complete all rank requirements
- [ ] Review the rank's skill requirements
- [ ] Be ready to discuss your camping experiences
- [ ] Have your scout handbook or advancement record ready
- [ ] Think about your leadership growth
- [ ] Prepare any questions you have

## Board of Review

### Purpose
A formal review to ensure requirements are met and to recognize achievement. Required for Star, Life, and Eagle ranks.

### What to Expect:
- Panel of 3-5 scouts and adults (not your Scoutmaster)
- They will ask about your experiences and understanding
- Questions may include:
  - Camping and outdoor experiences
  - Scout Oath and Law
  - Leadership experiences
  - Service projects
  - Personal growth and challenges

### How to Prepare:
- [ ] Complete ALL requirements before the Board
- [ ] Practice explaining your rank requirements
- [ ] Have your merit badge blue cards
- [ ] Bring evidence of camping nights
- [ ] Be ready to discuss your leadership
- [ ] Wear Class A uniform
- [ ] Be respectful and honest in your answers

### Tips for Success:
- Relax - they want to see you succeed!
- Answer questions thoroughly but concisely
- Show enthusiasm for Scouting
- Be honest about challenges and what you learned

---

**Key Points:**
- These are celebrations of your progress, not punishments
- Preparation shows respect and increases confidence

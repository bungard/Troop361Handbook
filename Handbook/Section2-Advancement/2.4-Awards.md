# 2.4 Special Awards & Recognition

## Order of the Arrow (OA)

### What is it?
The Order of the Arrow is Scouting's premier honor society, recognizing scouts and scouters who best exemplify the Scout Oath and Scout Law.

### Selection
- Scouts are nominated by their patrol
- Election is held annually
- Selection is based on character, outdoor skills, and service

### Benefits
- Recognition of achievement
- Additional training and fellowship opportunities
- Participation in OA events and conferences
- Lifetime membership

# 4.4.2 Cell Phones

Cell phone usage during a Scouting event can be an important tool as well as an undesired distraction. As a general guide, phones should be used solely as a tool to support the Scouting function.

**Policy Guidelines**

* **Leadership Authority:** The Scoutmaster and Senior Patrol Leader (SPL) maintain an operational cell phone policy for Scouts during all functions (meetings, campouts, etc.).
* **Expectation:** Scouts are required to follow this policy at all times.
* **Enforcement:** The Scoutmaster (or leader in charge) reserves the right to confiscate a Scout's phone if the Scout is unable or unwilling to follow the policy. If a family does not agree to this condition, the phone should remain at home.

---

**Confiscation Protocol**

If a phone is confiscated during an event:

1. **Parent Notification:** The Scoutmaster will notify the parents that the phone has been turned off and confiscated.
2. **Device Return:** The phone will be returned to the Scout or parents after the Scouting function ends.
3. **Emergency Contact:** Parents can reach their Scout during the function directly through the Scoutmaster.
4. **Dispute Resolution:** If parents feel the phone was unfairly confiscated, a discussion can take place with the Scoutmaster, Committee Chair, and parents after the function has concluded.

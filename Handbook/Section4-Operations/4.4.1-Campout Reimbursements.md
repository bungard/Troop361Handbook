# 4.4.1 Campout Reimbursements

Adult volunteers dedicate significant time and vehicle usage to support the troop program. This reimbursement policy exists to alleviate some of the direct expenses incurred by volunteers.

**Pricing and Transparency**
* **Cost Estimation:** Estimated reimbursement expenses should be factored into overall campout cost calculations based on expected drivers and participants. They are not intended as exact per-scout charges.
* **Component Itemization:** Campout costs should list individual cost components so scouts and families understand the event pricing.
* **Submission Process:** Individuals are responsible for submitting reimbursement requests via TroopWebHost. The Treasurer reserves the right to review and question any submission.

---

**Travel Reimbursements**

Travel reimbursements help offset driving expenses but are not intended to fully cover all driver expenditures.

* **Tolls:** Tolls incurred while driving to or from a campout are 100% reimbursable.
* **Trailer Towing:** The driver towing the troop trailer may claim **$0.50 per mile**.
* **Long-Distance Trips (100+ Miles Round Trip):** Drivers may be eligible for **$0.25 per mile** (the trailer-towing vehicle still receives $0.50 per mile). These eligible campouts must be identified in advance.

**Non-Reimbursable Travel Expenses**
* Parking tickets, traffic violations, or fines.
* Transportation provided strictly for individual convenience rather than the troop as a whole, including:
  * Driving a scout to a campout late due to personal conflicts (e.g., sporting events).
  * Picking up a scout early from an event.
  * Choosing to transport only your own scout and refusing to take other scout passengers.

---

**Scoutmaster & Assistant Scoutmaster Fees**

Troop 361 reimburses the primary leaders who provide required two-deep leadership for a campout (typically the Scoutmaster and an Assistant Scoutmaster).

* **Weekend Campouts:** Reimbursements are limited to camping fees and food costs.
* **Summer Camp:** Reimbursements cover full registration fees for the primary leaders. If the primary Scoutmaster or Assistant Scoutmaster cannot attend 100% of the time, the reimbursement will be prorated among the adults providing coverage.

### Meeting Structure

**Opening Circle (5-10 minutes)**
- Scouts gather around
- Scout Oath and Scout Law recitation
- Announcements for the evening

**Patrol Activities (30-45 minutes)**
- Patrols work on skills
- Games and competitions
- Merit badge work
- Leadership preparation

**Troop Activity (30-45 minutes)**
- All-troop games and activities
- Skill instruction
- Advancement opportunities
- Team building

**Closing Circle (5-10 minutes)**
- Gather around
- Reflect on the meeting
- Preview next week
- Thank participants

## Attendance Expectations

### Why Attendance Matters:
- Continuity of learning and advancement
- Team building and morale
- Leadership development
- Fun with friends!

### Reporting an Absence:
- Notify your Patrol Leader as soon as possible
- Let Scoutmaster know if you'll be away
- Make up any skills missed at next meeting

### Patrol Leader's Council (PLC)

The PLC meets weekly (or bi-weekly) before or after troop meetings:
- All Patrol Leaders and senior leadership attend
- Plan upcoming campouts and activities
- Discuss troop issues and improvements
- Make decisions affecting the troop
- Scouts of all ranks can attend as observers

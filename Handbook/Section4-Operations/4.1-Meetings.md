# 4.1 Meetings & Attendance

## Weekly Troop Meetings

### Meeting Schedule

**When:** Wednesday's 7pm-830pm
**Where:** first congregational church of tallmadge
**Duration:** Typically 1.5 - 2 hours

A complete Troop Calendar is maintained in (TroopWebHost)[https://t361.org]

### What to Bring
- [ ] Scout handbook
- [ ] Pencil or pen
- [ ] Class A or B uniform

### Meeting Structure

**Opening Circle (5-10 minutes)**
- Scouts gather around
- Scout Oath and Scout Law recitation
- Announcements for the evening

**Patrol Activities (30-45 minutes)**
- Patrols work on skills
- Games and competitions
- Merit badge work
- Leadership preparation

**Troop Activity (30-45 minutes)**
- All-troop games and activities
- Skill instruction
- Advancement opportunities
- Team building

**Closing Circle (5-10 minutes)**
- Gather around
- Reflect on the meeting
- Preview next week
- Close with a Scoutmaster's Minute

## Attendance Expectations

### Why Attendance Matters:
- Continuity of learning and advancement
- Team building and morale
- Leadership development
- Fun with friends!

### Reporting an Absence:
- Notify your Patrol Leader as soon as possible
- Make up any skills missed at next meeting

### Patrol Leader's Council (PLC)

The PLC meets typically the monday immediately after a campout. 
- All Patrol Leaders and senior leadership attend
- Plan upcoming campouts and activities
- Discuss troop issues and improvements
- Make decisions affecting the troop
- Only PLC members are expected to attend. 

### Summer & Holiday Breaks

The Troop will typically meeting throughout the year. There are exceptions (2-4 week break after Summer Camp, Monday's that coincide with Holidays, etc). The SPL and Scoutmaster will communicate the schedule, as will the Calendar in TroopWebHost.

---

**Key Points:**
- Consistency builds skills and camaraderie
- Missing meetings affects patrol and advancement
- Communication helps leaders plan better

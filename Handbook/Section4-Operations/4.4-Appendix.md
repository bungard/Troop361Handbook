## Knot Cheat Sheet

### Quick Reference

**Square Knot** - Joining ropes
- 1. Right over left
- 2. Left over right
- 3. Pull tight

**Bowline** - Fixed loop
- 1. Make rabbit hole (small loop)
- 2. Rabbit comes out of hole
- 3. Around the tree
- 4. Rabbit goes back down hole
- 5. Pull tight

**Clove Hitch** - Secure to pole
- 1. Wrap around pole downward
- 2. Cross over and wrap again
- 3. Wrap back over crossing point
- 4. Pull tight

**Taut-line Hitch** - Adjustable loop
- 1. Wrap around rope below loop
- 2. Wrap again inside the loop
- 3. Wrap again above the loop
- 4. Tighten knot and slide loop to desired position

**Two Half Hitches** - Quick attachment
- 1. Wrap around pole and cross over itself
- 2. Make first half hitch (overhand knot)
- 3. Make second half hitch below the first
- 4. Pull tight

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: How often should scouts attend meetings?
A: We expect scouts to attend weekly meetings. Missing meetings affects patrol morale and personal advancement.

### Q: Can a scout skip a campout?
A: Scouts can occasionally miss a campout, but regular attendance is important for skill development and bonding.

### Q: How much does it cost to be in Scouts?
A: Monthly dues are [amount], plus campout fees. Financial assistance is available for scouts who need it.

### Q: What if my scout doesn't have all the gear?
A: We have loaner equipment available. Talk to the Quartermaster or Committee Chair.

### Q: How can I get more involved?
A: Contact the Committee Chair - we need parent volunteers!

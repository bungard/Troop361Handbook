# 4.2 Code of Conduct & Youth Protection

## Scout Code of Conduct

### We Treat Each Other with Respect
- Use kind and appropriate language
- Listen when others are speaking
- Respect differences in opinions, abilities, and backgrounds
- Include everyone and don't leave scouts out
- Don't gossip or spread rumors

### We Follow Instructions
- Listen to adult leaders and follow their guidance
- Respect authority and chain of command
- Ask for clarification if you don't understand
- Complete assigned tasks responsibly

### We Are Honest
- Tell the truth, even when it's difficult
- Admit mistakes and learn from them
- Don't cheat or lie to get out of consequences
- Be truthful about your advancement progress

### We Keep Our Hands and Feet to Ourselves
- No hitting, pushing, or physical bullying
- No inappropriate touching
- Respect personal space
- Ask before borrowing someone's gear

### We Use Appropriate Language
- No swearing, profanity, or crude language
- No name-calling or insulting others
- No discriminatory or hateful speech
- Keep conversations respectful and positive

### We Make Responsible Choices
- No drugs, alcohol, or tobacco use
- No weapons (except approved scout tools)
- No stealing or vandalism
- Think before you act

## Digital Citizenship & Cyber Chip

### Online Safety
- Only interact with people you know in person
- Don't share personal information online
- Be aware that anything posted can be permanent
- Don't engage with strangers online

### Respectful Digital Behavior
- Don't cyberbully or send mean messages
- Don't impersonate others online
- Don't share embarrassing photos of others
- Remember: digital actions have real consequences

### Privacy & Security
- Use strong passwords
- Don't share passwords with anyone
- Know what information apps collect
- Adjust privacy settings on social media

### Appropriate Content
- Be thoughtful about what you post
- Don't share graphic or inappropriate content
- Respect copyright and don't share pirated content
- Think about how posts reflect on you and the troop

## Youth Protection Policies

Troop 361 prioritizes the safety and well-being of all scouts.

### Two-Deep Leadership
- Two registered adult leaders present at all troop activities
- One-on-one adult-scout interactions are avoided
- Exception: Scoutmaster conferences are private but within sight of other adults

### Appropriate Boundaries
- Adults do not have inappropriate physical contact with scouts
- Private spaces between adults and scouts are avoided
- When changing clothes, privacy screens or separate areas are used
- All activities are age-appropriate

### Reporting Concerns
If you see something that makes you uncomfortable:
- Talk to a trusted adult immediately
- Tell your parents or guardians
- Contact the Scoutmaster or Committee Chair
- You can also call the Council to report concerns
- Your safety is the priority - speak up!

### Scout Abuse Prevention Training
- All leaders are trained in youth protection
- Leaders understand appropriate boundaries
- Resources are available to scouts who need help

---

**Key Points:**
- Respect is the foundation of our troop culture
- We all have a responsibility to keep everyone safe
- Speaking up about concerns helps protect everyone
- The Cyber Chip helps you stay safe online

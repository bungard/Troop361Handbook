# 3.2 Leave No Trace (LNT)

## The 7 Principles of Leave No Trace

Leave No Trace is an ethical framework for enjoying the outdoors responsibly and minimizing environmental impact.

### 1. Plan Ahead and Prepare
- Study the area before you go
- Know the regulations and restrictions
- Be prepared for emergencies and bad weather
- Avoid traveling during sensitive times (breeding season, muddy seasons)

### 2. Travel and Camp on Durable Surfaces
- Stay on established trails
- Camp on designated campsites or resilient surfaces (rock, gravel)
- Avoid camping on vegetation or soft soil
- Walk single file on trails to avoid widening them

### 3. Dispose of Waste Properly
- Pack it in, pack it out - take all trash with you
- Human waste must be buried 200 feet from water sources
- Use established toilet facilities when available
- Never leave food scraps or washing water in natural areas

### 4. Leave What You Find
- Don't collect plants, rocks, or artifacts
- Don't carve initials into trees
- Don't rearrange natural objects
- Leave the area as pristine as you found it for others to enjoy

### 5. Minimize Campfire Impact
- Use a camp stove instead of fires when possible
- Keep fires small and in a designated ring
- Fully extinguish fires before leaving
- Pack out all ash and unburned wood
- Consider fire-free camping in high-use areas

### 6. Respect Wildlife
- Observe animals from a distance
- Never feed wild animals
- Store food securely to avoid attracting animals
- Don't remove animals from the wild
- Avoid making loud noises that disturb wildlife

### 7. Be Considerate of Other Visitors
- Yield to others on trails
- Keep noise levels low
- Camp away from other groups when possible
- Leave no signs of your presence

## LNT Applied to Troop Campouts

[How Troop 361 specifically applies these principles]

- All scouts must follow LNT practices
- Trash bags are carried out from every campout
- Established campsites are used whenever possible
- Camp stoves are preferred over campfires
- Wildlife observation is encouraged but from a safe distance

---

**Key Points:**
- Leave No Trace protects the outdoors for everyone
- It's not just the environment - it's respect for others
- Small actions by many scouts add up to significant impact

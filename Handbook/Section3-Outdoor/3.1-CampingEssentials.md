# 3.1 Camping Essentials

## The Ten Essentials

These items should be in your backpack on every outdoor adventure:

1. **Navigation** - Map, compass, GPS device
2. **Sun Protection** - Sunscreen, sunglasses, hat
3. **Insulation** - Extra layers, warm jacket, hat
4. **Illumination** - Flashlight, headlamp, extra batteries
5. **First Aid Supplies** - Personal medications, blister kit, basic first aid
6. **Fire** - Waterproof matches, lighter, emergency fire starter
7. **Repair Kit** - Knife, multi-tool, duct tape, cord
8. **Nutrition** - Extra food beyond expected needs
9. **Hydration** - Water bottle or hydration system
10. **Emergency Shelter** - Bivy sack, emergency blanket, or tarp

## Weekend Campout Packing Checklist

### Clothing
- [ ] Comfortable hiking clothes
- [ ] Extra socks and underwear
- [ ] Warm layers (fleece or down jacket)
- [ ] Rain jacket or poncho
- [ ] Hat
- [ ] Sturdy closed-toe hiking boots
- [ ] Camp shoes or sandals
- [ ] Scout uniform (Class A)

### Shelter & Sleep
- [ ] Sleeping bag (rated for season)
- [ ] Sleeping pad or foam pad
- [ ] Pillow (optional)
- [ ] Tent assignment or shelter assignment

### Hygiene & Health
- [ ] Toothbrush and toothpaste
- [ ] Soap and washcloth
- [ ] Towel
- [ ] Deodorant
- [ ] Any personal medications
- [ ] Toilet paper and hand sanitizer

### Other Items
- [ ] Water bottle (1-2 liters)
- [ ] Flashlight or headlamp
- [ ] Notebook and pencil
- [ ] Scout handbook
- [ ] Spending money for any activities

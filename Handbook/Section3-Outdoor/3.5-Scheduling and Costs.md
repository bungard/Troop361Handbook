# 3.5 Scheduling and Costs

## Scheduling

**Campout Themes & Locations**
* **Planning Sessions:** Semi-annually (during Summer Camp and after the January campout), the Scouts hold planning sessions to select campout locations and themes for the following year. 
* **Scout-Led Process:** The Scouts determine the purpose and destination of all campouts. Adult advice may be sought, but adult direction should not drive the selections.
* **Lead Time:** Decisions are made early to allow sufficient lead time for site reservations and itinerary planning.

**Calendar & Timing**
* **Committee Responsibility:** The Troop Committee sets specific campout dates, taking into account school, holiday, and community schedules to minimize conflicts.
* **Approval Timeline:** The Committee approves the upcoming program year calendar (September through July) no later than May, and ideally by April.
* **Schedule Integrity:** Once published, rescheduling should be strictly avoided except for unavoidable issues, such as facility unavailability or critical adult leader shortages.

---

## Costs

> *"A Scout is thrifty."*

**Pricing & Budget Management**
* **Fee Notification:** The Troop Committee determines campout fees and notifies the Scoutmaster at least **4 weeks** in advance of the event. Rough costs are developed during initial calendar planning, and final pricing is set once site fees and activity costs are locked in.
* **General Fund Balance:** Minor variations between collected fees and actual costs are absorbed or credited by the Troop General Fund. Significant variances must be reviewed directly by the Scoutmaster, Committee Chair, and Treasurer.
* **Financial Reporting:** A monthly financial report is provided to the Committee for ongoing review.

---

## Cost Formula

Campout fees directly reflect the true expenses incurred by the troop.

$$\text{Campout Fee} = \text{Campsite Cost} + \text{Food Costs} + \text{Activity Costs} + \text{Reimbursement Costs}$$

* **Campsite Cost:** Estimated cost per participant. For group flat-rate sites (e.g., cabin rentals), the total cost is divided among expected participants to determine individual shares.
* **Food Cost:** Budgeted food allocation for the event duration. The standard base budget is **$15 per person** for a typical weekend campout (Friday Cracker Barrel through Sunday breakfast), adjusted up or down based on the number of meals provided.
* **Activity Costs:** Entry fees, equipment rentals, or special activity costs specific to the event.
* **Reimbursement Costs:** Factored into the calculation to help offset volunteer driving expenses. Specific guidelines are detailed in [Section 4.4.1 Campout Reimbursements](#441-campout-reimbursements).

# 3.5 Scheduling and Costs

## Scheduling

### Campout themes and location:
Semi-Annually (at Summer Camp and After the January Campout) the Scouts of the Troop will hold a planning session to determine where they would like to camp in the subsequent year. 
This planning is done in a timely fashion, to provide logistical means to arrange reservations and build out campout iteneraries. 

The Scouts will determine the purpose of all campouts and their location. Adult advisement can be sought, but should never be required. 

### Timing: 
The Troop Committee will retain the responsibility of determing the dates of specific campouts. This should try to take under consideration School, Holiday, and other notable schedules. Conflicts will occur (Band concerts, Sports Competitions, etc) and families are asked to be part of the planning to avoid as many conflicts as possible. 

No later than May of each year, the Committee will approve the Calendar for the following year (Sept of the current Year -> July of the next year). Ideally this takes place as early as April.

Once the schedule is set, the Troop should avoid rescheduling whenever possible. Excusable reschedules could include: unavailability fo desired activity, availability of Adult leaders, etc. 


# Costs
A scout is thrifty.

The Troop committee shall determine the cost of each campout and notify the Scoutmaster at least 4 weeks in advance of that campout. Ideally, rough costs are determined during calendar planning and final costs are determined once logistics (Site reservation and actiity fees are known). 

The Committe will try to balance the expenditures and fees of a campout. Differences will be absorbed into or by the Troop General fund. Excessive differences should be addressed directly by the Scoutmaster, Committee Chair, and Treasurer. 
A monthly report should be made available to the committee to review. 

## Cost Formula:
Campout costs should direclty reflect the costs incurred by the Troop to facilitate the campout. 

At its Core, the Formula is:
Campsite Cost + Food Costs + Activity Cost + Transportation Cost + Share of Adult Leader Fee = Campout Fee

### Campsite Cost 
Estimated cost per person for camping. 
In some cases this is provided by the site. In cases where the cost as based upon the group (ex: Cabin rental), an estimated share will be determined. 

### Food Cost
Food cost is a budgeted amount for food for the duration of the event. 
As of 2026, we budget $15 for a weekend campout (Friday Crakerbarrel through Sunday Breakfast). 

If an event requires additional meals, the cost may be increased. The same holds true if less meals are involved.

# 3.3 Outdoor Skills Refresher

## Essential Knots

Every scout should know how to tie these knots and understand when to use them:

### Square Knot
**Used for:** Joining two ropes of equal diameter
**When:** Binding bundles, first aid, general tying

### Bowline
**Used for:** Creating a fixed loop that won't slip
**When:** Securing rope to objects, emergency situations, rescue

### Clove Hitch
**Used for:** Securing rope to a pole or tree
**When:** Hitching to posts, setting up camp gear

### Taut-line Hitch
**Used for:** Adjustable loop that holds under tension
**When:** Tent guy-lines, securing loads

### Two Half Hitches
**Used for:** Quick secure attachment
**When:** Hitching to posts, securing loose items

## Fire Building

### Fire Safety First:
- Only build fires in designated rings or stoves
- Never leave a fire unattended
- Completely extinguish fires before sleeping or leaving
- Know local fire regulations and bans

### Building a Campfire:

1. **Gather materials:**
   - Tinder (dry leaves, lint, small twigs)
   - Kindling (pencil-sized sticks)
   - Fuel wood (wrist-thick logs)

2. **Build the structure:**
   - Place tinder in center
   - Arrange kindling in teepee or log cabin style
   - Have fuel wood ready nearby

3. **Light the fire:**
   - Light tinder with match or lighter
   - Gradually add kindling as it catches
   - Add larger fuel wood as fire grows

4. **Maintain the fire:**
   - Keep it small for cooking, warmth
   - Add wood as needed to control size
   - Use long sticks to manage fire safely

### Complete Extinguishment:
- Stir ashes with stick
- Pour water on coals multiple times
- Mix ashes and soil
- Feel with back of hand - must be cool

## Navigation

### Map Reading:
- Understand map symbols and legends
- Use compass to orient map to terrain
- Identify landmarks
- Plan routes using contour lines

### Compass Use:
- Set compass for bearing
- Align compass with map
- Use orienting lines to find direction
- Take bearings on landmarks

### Trail Navigation:
- Follow blazes (painted marks) on trees
- Stay on established trails
- Use landmarks for confirmation
- Know when to turn back if lost

### GPS Use:
- Know how to use GPS devices as backup
- Never rely solely on GPS
- Traditional navigation skills are primary

## Knife Safety - Totin' Chip

### Knife Handling Rules:
1. Always cut AWAY from your body
2. Pass knife handle-first to others
3. Keep knife sharp - sharp blades are safer
4. Use knife only for intended purposes
5. Clean and dry your knife after use
6. Carry knife safely with blade pointing down
7. Never play with a knife
8. Store knife safely away from others
9. Never throw your knife
10. Always ask permission before using someone else's knife

### Totin' Chip Requirements:
- Demonstrate knife safety knowledge
- Show proper knife handling
- Pass knife safety test
- Wear your Totin' Chip with pride!
